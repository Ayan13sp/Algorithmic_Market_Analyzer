## Headline

* Out-of-sample balanced accuracy ranges from **0.495 to 0.528** across 5 stocks (chance = 0.500).
* **0/5** stocks beat their majority-class baseline at the 5% level (exact McNemar test).
* **1/5** strategies beat random timing at the 5% level (permutation test, same number of trades); **0/5** have a net Sharpe ratio whose 95% CI is above zero.
* The median gross edge per trade is **-2.1 bps** against realistic round-trip costs of 12.8 bps.
* Drift monitor right now: **2** alert(s) and **1** warning(s) across 5 stocks (section 3).
